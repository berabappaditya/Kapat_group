AR

Dr. Ananya Raghunathan

Principal Investigator
CurrentSince 2016

Ananya leads the group's efforts in earth-abundant metal catalysis. After a PhD at IISc Bangalore and postdoctoral work at ETH Zürich, she returned to India to build a lab asking a single stubborn question: can cheap metals be taught to do noble-metal chemistry? Her work spans mechanistic electrochemistry, ligand design, and in-situ spectroscopy.

Postdoc
ETH Zürich, Switzerland
Current Position
Associate Professor · Dept. of Chemistry
VK

Vikram Kulkarni

PhD Scholar
CurrentSince 2022

Vikram works at the intersection of photoredox catalysis and flow chemistry, designing continuous processes for late-stage C–H functionalization. He believes the best reactions are the ones you can run on a rooftop with sunlight — and he has the reactor prototypes to prove it. Off-hours he is the lab's unofficial coffee chemist and a competitive badminton player.

M.Sc.
IIT Bombay, India
Current Focus
Photoredox flow catalysis
MF

Meera Fernandes

Project Associate
CurrentSince 2024

Meera handles the group's computational arm, running DFT studies that explain why reactions work — or, more often, why they refuse to. Trained in theoretical chemistry at the University of Hyderabad, she is building the group's first machine-learned reactivity models and maintains a healthy skepticism of any barrier below 5 kcal/mol.

M.Sc.
University of Hyderabad, India
Current Focus
DFT & ML reactivity models
SS

Shreya Sivaramakrishnan

B.Sc. (Research) Chemistry
Alumni2021–25

Shreya pursued her B.Sc. (Research) in Chemistry with a passion for scientific inquiry and sustainable development. Her research journey began in high school, leading her through electrochemistry, computational biology, and chemical biology across multiple internships and projects. As an OUR student she worked on metalloradical-based reduction reactions, and her final thesis focused on trifluoromethylation. Beyond the lab, she enjoys movies, connecting with people, and finding solace in nature treks and photography.

“Research is not just about finding answers; it's about asking the right questions that lead to a sustainable future.”
Internship
California Institute of Technology, USA
Current Position
PhD Student · Caltech, USA

Rahul Joshi

PhD, 2023
Alumni2018–23

Rahul's thesis established the group's nickel-hydride platform for alkene isomerization, work that produced three first-author papers and one memorably stubborn air-sensitive complex. A patient mentor to four batches of undergraduates, he set the standard for how the group documents its chemistry.
